## About me

I’m a Research Software Engineer at the [University of Birmingham](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/). I help researchers build and use software, data tools and computing resources.

Before that, I worked at the University of Warwick as a Senior RSE and Academic Technologist. I built research software, taught workshops and supported research teams.
